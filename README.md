# 3daigamesolo

A first-person parkour game in Unity 6 (6000.4.0f1), styled after the Pururin world from J's World: flat pastel cel shading and ink outlines.

**Levels** (Tab switches between them):

- **Akiba** (`Assets/Scenes/Akiba.unity`, the game opens here): an Akihabara night in a PlayStation style (drawn at a low resolution, wobbly vertices, warped point-sampled textures, 5-bit colour with dithering). A 960 m main street packed with buildings covered in neon (vertical signs over the street, flickering sign bands, scrolling LED tickers, billboards with anime art, graffiti), shop stalls, vending and capsule toy machines, traffic both ways, side streets strung with power lines, an elevated railway with Akihabara station and a train, flying cars, skyscrapers, giant lucky cats, a blimp, and Jet Set Radio-style rails everywhere: curving up from kickers to balconies, across the street, spiralling round pillars. The route it's built round: from the start, kick off the ramp onto the curving rail, grind it onto a balcony, ride the sign ramp up to the roof, hop on the flying car, ride it up the skyscraper's face, wall-run along the skyscraper onto the pipe up its corner, and power-grind (hold Shift) into the power lines over the whole city. Textures are generated by `Tools/make_akiba_textures.py` (real Japanese sign text from the Windows fonts); the level is built by `Assets/Editor/SkateWorldBuilder.Akiba.cs`.
- **Sakura City** (`Assets/Scenes/SakuraCity.unity`): a small Japanese city in the same pastel toon style, lighter. A ring road with cars and buses going round (ride on their roofs), an elevated railway over the main boulevard with a train running back and forth through a station, power lines on poles all round the ring and up to rooftops, guard rails, handrails and rooftop edges to grind, shop streets with awnings, vending machines and alleys, a parking garage to skate up, a skate plaza, cherry blossom drifting about, flickering signs and spinning rooftop fans. The goal is the top of the tower in the middle (58 m), and there's more than one way up: skate up the garage and grind the power line to the 22 m roof beside the tower, or wall-jump up the alley between them, or take the train route (station stairs, the train's roof, the canopy, across the rooftops); then climb a billboard frame onto the tower's terrace (30 m), and the staggered balconies or the wall-jump shaft to the top. Built by `Assets/Editor/SkateWorldBuilder.City.cs`.
- **Pink Palace** (`Assets/Scenes/PinkPalace.unity`): a Y2K pink palace on a floating island in deep space. Glittery halftone tiles, a grand staircase up to a terrace, a fountain, ramps, ledges, giant strawberries, floating hearts and a ringed planet. Run out through a gap in the railing and you fall into space and start again. Off the west side, over a bridge, is **the tower**: a spiral ramp (skate it) round a pink core to 14 m, a wall-jump shaft to 30 m, another to 46 m, then stepping stones spiralling up to a crown at 56 m, with a checkpoint at each level. Out of the gap on the east side runs the **space course**: floating platforms, stepping stones and pillars climbing to a goal under a giant star, with checkpoints (hearts on poles) along the way. Built by `Assets/Editor/SkateWorldBuilder.Palace.cs`.
- **Pururin Valley** (`Assets/Scenes/SkatePark.unity`): the pastel valley, a long run ending in a plaza with ramps, ledges and a big curved wall. The ground is currently flat (`FlatGround` in `SkateWorldBuilder.cs`; set it to false and rebuild for the downhill valley).

Rails, power lines and ledges can be ground on the skateboard.

## Controls

First person (press **V** for third person). Gravity is much heavier than Unity's default, and heavier still on the way down, so jumps are quick and don't hang.

| Key | |
|---|---|
| **Mouse** | look |
| **WASD** | run (about 23 km/h); you start, stop and turn almost instantly, and have nearly full control in the air |
| **Shift** | sprint (about 40 km/h) |
| **Space** | jump: tap for a short one, **hold for a higher one**. Coyote time (you can still jump just after running off an edge) and jump buffering (press just before landing and you jump on touchdown). Hopping again and again the same way slowly builds speed. **In the air against a wall, Space wall jumps** off it: bounce between two walls to climb a shaft (you can't climb one wall on its own). Pressing into a wall while falling slides you down it slowly. **Wall run**: hit a wall at speed in the air and you run (or ride) along it |
| **C** | slide: a burst of speed, keeps your momentum, and you fit under low things (barriers, bars); jump out of it to go higher. On the board it's a powerslide: carve hard without losing speed, and come out of it faster |
| **Skateboard** | it's always with you: you start each level riding it. Without Shift you cruise (about 43 km/h); hold Shift and you go flat out (about 108 km/h). Kickers launch you. Hold Shift on a rail to power grind (full speed, even uphill). You stay on it however you land. **Right click** (or Q) steps off, carrying it, for running and wall jumps; right click again to drop back on. Jump onto a rail or power line on the board to grind it (A/D switches grind; Space jumps off). Only really big air kickflips it, and only sometimes |
| **V** | switch between first and third person |
| **Tab** | next level |
| **R** | back to the last checkpoint (or the start) |
| **Esc / click** | free / recapture the mouse |

Tuning lives on the Player object's `SlideController`, grouped into Look, Third person, Running, Jumping, Wall jump, Slide, Air and Skateboard.

## Rebuilding the world

The scenes are generated by `Assets/Editor/SkateWorldBuilder.cs` and `SkateWorldBuilder.Palace.cs` (menu **Skate > Build world**). Change the layout there and rebuild rather than editing the scenes by hand. **Skate > Run test runs** steps the real controller and physics through scripted runs (running and stopping, jump height and air control, held jumps, coyote time, jump buffering, hop speed, sliding, solid ground everywhere, the palace stairs) and logs PASS/FAIL to the console.

Command line:

```
Unity -batchmode -projectPath . -executeMethod SkateWorldBuilder.BuildFromCommandLine   # build both levels + tests + screenshots in Review/
Unity -batchmode -projectPath . -executeMethod SkateWorldBuilder.BuildPlayer            # Windows build in Builds/Staging
```

The shaders in `Assets/Shaders` are adapted from J's World's Pururin world shaders. The HUD font is Bangers (SIL Open Font License, see `Assets/Resources/Bangers - OFL.txt`).
